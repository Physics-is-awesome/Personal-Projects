\section{From Liouville to BBGKY}

Taking an N-body Hamiltonian

\[
H = \frac{1}{2m}\sum^N_{i=1} \va{p_i}^2 + \sum^N_{i=1} V\va{r_i} + \sum^N_{i<j} U(\va{r_i} - \va{r_j})
  \]

  Where the Hamiltonian constains an external force $\va{F} = - \nabla V$, that acts equally on every particle. There is also two-body interaction between particles captured by the potential energy term. Later on we will make the assumption that this potential is short ranged.
