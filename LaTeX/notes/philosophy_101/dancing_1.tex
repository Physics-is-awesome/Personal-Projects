\section{Dancing With the Devil by Krista K. Thomason}

\subsection{Introduction}

The author suggests a dichotomy between the students of nature and that of dialect. Why is that, they both lead to the same results. She frames it as if students of nature continually refer to it in dismissing concern, when this is by definition not true.

I did like the development on the connection between head and heart, but right now it is far too simplistic. Very few people refer to those of reason to be without opinion or axioms, but rather someone consistent with their axioms regardless of the situation. A way to articulate this is someone who would act the same in a hypothetical and in the situation.
